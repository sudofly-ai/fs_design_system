Hero rules of thumb

Pick one temperature

One gradient phrase

Exactly one span of `.fs-text-gradient` per headline. Never the whole line.

One primary CTA

Magenta button appears once. Second action is always `.fs-btn-secondary` or ghost.

Don't stack backgrounds

Mesh OR grid OR full-bleed — never two at once. The background is a temperature, not a collage.
