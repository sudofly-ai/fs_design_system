States

Rest · Hover · Loading · Disabled

Hover lifts 1px and brightens the glow. Loading replaces the label with a spinner — the button keeps its width. Disabled drops saturation and opacity; never use disabled as a "coming soon" signal.

Usage rules

Three non-negotiables

One primary per view

The magenta button appears exactly once on any given surface. Secondary actions demote to outline or ghost.

Imperative, specific labels

"Start Free", "Connect Lovable", "Schedule a Demo." Never "Click here", "Submit", or "Learn more".

Never stack two primaries

If you need two equally-weighted CTAs, one is wrong. Find the real primary; make the other secondary.
