The FutureStack Mark

An isometric cube inside a circle — the three visible faces echo the three brand accents (cyan, magenta, purple) and the concentric rings read as a signal being emitted. It's a container, and it's transmitting.

Primary mark

FutureStack

The cube-in-circle is the canonical brand mark. Prefer the full-color version on navy surfaces. Always paired with the wordmark at 1× mark height (see clearspace).

Clearspace

One mark height, every side

The logo breathes. Reserve space equal to the mark's own height on every side — no text, no other marks, no decorative elements.

1× 1× 1× 1×

The 1× rule

Measure the mark's height. Reserve that same distance around every edge. In a lockup with the wordmark, the wordmark sits at 1× mark height, aligned to the center of the cube.
